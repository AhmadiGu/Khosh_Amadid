افغانستان

Afghanistan sits where Central and South Asia meet, in the folds of the Hindu Kush. Its cities were stops on the Silk Road for thousands of years, and its valleys hold blue lakes, tiled mosques, orchards and a tall brick minaret. Start with a place, a dish or a custom, then see what the year looks like.
